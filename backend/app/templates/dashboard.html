What is PulseCheck, and how do I use it?

In plain English

Think of it like a heartbeat monitor — but for your automated tasks instead of a person. Every time your script or backup job finishes, it sends a tiny "I'm alive" signal. If that signal doesn't show up when it's supposed to, PulseCheck taps you on the shoulder and says "hey, something didn't run."

How to use it

Create a monitor above, copy its link from the monitor's detail page, and add one line to the end of your job: curl -fsS <your-link>. That's the entire setup.

Why you'd want this

Most monitoring tools watch things that are already running. Nothing watches for a job that never started, or a script that crashed before finishing — that's the gap PulseCheck fills.

How it's different from other tools

Same core idea as Healthchecks.io or Cronitor (both bigger, paid platforms) — self-hostable and fully open here, so you can read every line of what happens to your data. It doesn't replace Prometheus/Grafana, it complements them: they watch live services, PulseCheck watches jobs that only run occasionally.
