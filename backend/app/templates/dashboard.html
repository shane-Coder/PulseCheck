What is PulseCheck, and how do I use it?

What it does

PulseCheck is a "dead man's switch" for scheduled jobs. Your cron job, backup script, or scheduled task pings a unique URL every time it finishes successfully. If a ping doesn't arrive within the expected window, we assume it silently failed and email you — before you find out the hard way, days later, that backups stopped running.

How to use it

Create a monitor above, copy its ping URL from the monitor's detail page, and add one line to the end of your job: curl -fsS <ping-url>. That's the entire integration.

Why you'd want this

Most monitoring (Prometheus, UptimeRobot, etc.) watches things that are already running. Nothing watches for a cron job that never started, or a script that crashed before finishing — that's the gap PulseCheck fills.

How it's different from other tools

Same core idea as Healthchecks.io or Cronitor, but self-hostable and fully open — read every line of what happens to your ping. It doesn't replace Prometheus/Grafana, it complements them: they watch live services, PulseCheck watches jobs that only run occasionally.
